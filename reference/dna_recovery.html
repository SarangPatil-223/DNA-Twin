Why they diverge: Church hits the theoretical max density (2 bits/base) but ignores biology, so it can produce long repeated-base runs. Goldman guarantees no repeats — each base depends on the one before it — which costs density and means a single error can cascade, so its recovery accuracy tends to fall faster as the error rate climbs.
